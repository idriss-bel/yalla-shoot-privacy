Frequently asked questions

  • Today's matches aren't showing? Check your internet connection and pull down to refresh.
  • Not receiving goal alerts? Make sure notifications are enabled for the app in your device settings, and the team is added to your favorites.
  • Stream links aren't working? Stream links are provided by third parties; we don't host them. Try another server from the list if one is available.
